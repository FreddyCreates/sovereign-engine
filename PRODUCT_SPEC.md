# PRODUCT SPECIFICATION: Sovereign Engine — AI Monetization & Growth OS for App Developers

**Target Event:** RevenueCat Shipaton 2026  
**Core Mission:** Help Apps Make More Money across existing global app marketplaces.  
**Team Entry:** ItsnotAIlabs & ThyFriendlyFox [RGNT]  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Product Pitch

**Sovereign Engine** is an **AI-Powered Monetization & Autonomous App Generator OS** designed for app developers, creators, and publishers shipping to existing global marketplaces (Apple App Store, Google Play Store, Samsung Galaxy Store, and Web/Stripe). 

It embeds **Gemini AI** directly into the platform so users do not have to code piece-by-piece: in a **single autonomous session**, Gemini AI manufactures full-stack applications, Motoko smart contracts, RevenueCat product offerings, Paywalls v2 layouts, and international Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) localization.

---

## 2. Friction Points & Problem Statement

| App Developer Friction | How Sovereign Engine Solves It |
|---|---|
| **Low Paywall Conversion** | Multi-agent AI automatically tests RevenueCat Paywall v2 variants to maximize ARPU. |
| **High International Churn** | Dynamic PPP pricing automatically localizes subscription rates for US, Europe, Asia, & LatAm. |
| **Subscriber Refunds & Cancellation** | RevenueCat Customer Center self-service with automated AI retention discounts & win-back campaigns. |
| **Fragmented Store Management** | Unified dashboard aggregating revenue across App Store, Google Play, Galaxy Store, & Web/Stripe. |
| **Complex Subscription Setup** | 1-line integration with RevenueCat REST API v2, Webhooks, and OneSignal engagement. |

---

## 3. Integrated Marketplaces

Instead of creating a isolated exchange, **Sovereign Engine** integrates existing, world-class app marketplaces:
1. **Apple App Store** (iOS / iPadOS / macOS StoreKit)
2. **Google Play Store** (Android Billing)
3. **Samsung Galaxy Store** (Galaxy In-App Purchase)
4. **Stripe & Web Funnels** (RevenueCat Funnels)

---

## 4. What the App User (Developer / Publisher) Does

1. **Connect Marketplaces**: Connect RevenueCat Project API key to instantly aggregate real-time MRR, ARR, and subscriber lifecycle events.
2. **Deploy AI Paywalls**: Select from RevenueCat Paywalls v2 templates optimized for high conversion.
3. **Run AI Retention Defense**: Configure Customer Center self-service paths that offer targeted discounts when subscribers attempt to cancel.
4. **Track Growth & Virality**: Monitor A/B test experiments, OneSignal campaign metrics, and #BuildInPublic social reach.
