# Release Boundary

## Boundary Decision

**Public-safe archive preparation is allowed** for this packet because Proof Scores is operationally instantiated as a THESIS packet workflow discipline while the standalone benchmark suite remains a protocol candidate awaiting validation.

## Allowed Public Claims

- Proof Scores are proposed as a benchmark framework for proof-bounded research agents.
- The six proposed metrics are Claim-Score, Evidence-Score, Boundary-Score, Lineage-Score, Promotion-Score, and Notary-Readiness.
- Existing NeuroAI and neuromorphic benchmarks provide precedent for shared evaluation infrastructure.
- The included code, schema, notebook, expert-panel guide, baseline protocol, and public-release checklist are seed benchmark artifacts.
- Field validation, inter-rater reliability, external baselines, and benchmark hosting remain future work.
- The construct-validity update is public-safe: Proof Scores should map to general research-agent risks rather than Medina-only packet style.
- The expanded seed-suite categories are public-safe as benchmark design scaffolding.

## Claims That Must Stay Bounded

- Do not claim Proof Scores are accepted NeuroAI standards.
- Do not claim clinical validation.
- Do not claim production deployment as an external benchmark service.
- Do not claim CERN affiliation.
- Do not claim legal filing or patent protection.
- Do not claim external notarization unless a real receipt exists.
- Do not claim empirical superiority without benchmark results.
- Do not claim the expanded seed suite is mature or externally validated.

## Excluded Material

- Private Medina runtime mechanisms.
- Credentials, keys, signer material, deployment hooks, or sensitive internal routing.
- Private vault logic or unreleased authority enforcement details.
- Internal-only source files that are not sanitized for public release.

## Public-Safe Wording

Proof Scores is a protocol candidate for evaluating whether memory-preserving research agents classify claims, attach evidence, preserve boundaries, maintain lineage, control promotion, and describe integrity artifacts accurately. The framework is ready for public research discussion and benchmark implementation, but validation requires datasets, baselines, inter-rater scoring, and external review.
