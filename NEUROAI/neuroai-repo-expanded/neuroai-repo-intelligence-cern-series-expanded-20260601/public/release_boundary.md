# Release Boundary

## Public-Safe Draft Claims

- Medina repo intelligence can be framed as a NeuroAI research substrate for memory-preserving, proof-bounded, authority-aware agents.
- `NOVA_ROOT`, `MEMORY_RUNTIME`, `ACTIVE_STATE_RUNTIME`, and `FOUNDATION_FLOOR` define an architecture that can be studied as a memory runtime hypothesis.
- Source/Forge/Deploy/Nexus can be studied as a cognitive lifecycle for artifact-producing agents.
- THESIS proof discipline can be proposed as a benchmark suite for claim-making agents.
- Auro can be treated as Medina’s native speaking-intelligence research surface.

## Blocked Claims

- Biological equivalence or validated brain model.
- Clinical or regulatory validity.
- Deployed complete NeuroAI platform.
- Runtime-law proof or verified denial-path enforcement.
- Legal filing, patent protection, CERN affiliation, or external notarization.
- Auro sentience, consciousness, human-equivalence, or proven emotional safety.

## Required Wording

Use: “repo-derived hypothesis,” “protocol candidate,” “benchmark proposal,” “research surface,” “public-safe draft,” “operator review required,” and “local hash only.”

Avoid: “proven,” “solved,” “validated,” “deployment-ready,” “brain-equivalent,” “notarized,” “CERN affiliated,” and “legally protected.”
