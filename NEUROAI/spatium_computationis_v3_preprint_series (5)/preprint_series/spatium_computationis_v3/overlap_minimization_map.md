# Overlap Minimization Map

Goal: preserve shared lineage while preventing each preprint from repeating the same mathematics, architecture claims, or implementation assertions.

## Shared Text Budget

Each paper may reuse:

- One paragraph defining Spatium Computationis v3.
- One paragraph placing the paper within the four-part series.
- One short source-lineage note.
- One compact limitations paragraph referencing evidence gaps.

Each paper should avoid:

- Copying the full original abstract.
- Repeating all four canonical contributions.
- Reusing the same conclusion claims.
- Restating all equations from other papers.

## Shared Lineage Citation Pattern

Recommended internal citation note:

## Anti-Overlap Reviewer Checklist

- Does the paper make one primary contribution?
- Are equations copied only when they are owned by the paper?
- Are implementation claims stated only where evidence is expected?
- Does the abstract avoid listing all four series contributions?
- Does the conclusion avoid claiming the whole system is validated?
- Are private or security-sensitive details omitted unless this is Paper 4 and release-approved?
