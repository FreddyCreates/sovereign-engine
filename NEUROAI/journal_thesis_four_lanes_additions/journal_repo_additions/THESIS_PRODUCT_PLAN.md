Product name: **THESIS**

Product class: **Research Proof Operating System**

Core line: **THESIS verifies research before the world believes it.**

## Product Definition

THESIS is a research proof operating system that turns papers, repos, drafts, claims, logs, and evidence into bounded research packets.

It answers:

- What is real?
- What is asserted?
- What is missing?
- What is publishable?
- What needs evidence?
- What should be protected?
- What can be printed, hashed, archived, or submitted?

## Four Product Lanes

| Lane | User | Job | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GPT Marketplace THESIS | Researchers, builders, students, founders | Turn research material into proof-bounded packets | Claims matrix, evidence map, paper outline, PDF plan, release boundary |
| Julia Terminal THESIS | Technical researchers, labs, repo-heavy builders | Audit local repos and generate packet artifacts from terminal | Repo scan, proof ledger, missing evidence queue, local hashes |
| THESIS Hub | Research teams and solo operators | Manage papers, repos, claims, PDFs, manifests, and review states | Workspace dashboard, artifact library, print/export surface |
| Verifier Mode | Funders, reviewers, accelerators, journals, labs | Check external research claims against visible evidence | Verification report, risk score, proof gaps, diligence packet |

## Why This Is Valuable

Most research tooling helps people write. THESIS helps people prove.

The strategic wedge is repo and claim verification. A researcher may have dozens of repositories, drafts, and experiments. A funder or reviewer may need to know whether a claim is supported by implementation evidence. THESIS creates an independent evidence layer that does not rely on the repository host's own AI as the final authority.

## Product Principles

1. Never let a claim outrun its evidence.
2. Treat repositories as evidence surfaces, not decoration.
3. Separate proof, publication, IP, and deployment.
4. Produce printable artifacts by default.
5. Hash serious outputs before release.
6. Keep public-safe summaries separate from private mechanisms.

## Authority States

- `DRAFT`: idea or rough packet.
- `CLAIM_HARDENED`: claims classified and bounded.
- `REPO_VERIFIED_PARTIAL`: repository evidence inspected.
- `HASHED`: local hash manifest exists.
- `PRINT_READY`: printable PDF/DOCX exists.
- `PUBLIC_SAFE`: private-core and IP checks complete.
- `VERIFIER_READY`: suitable for third-party diligence.
