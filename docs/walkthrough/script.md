# Sovereign Books walkthrough script

Target viewer: a founder running a 5 to 20 person software company who
currently pays for QuickBooks and a bookkeeper, and who has never claimed
a research credit. Total runtime about two and a half minutes. Each stage
is one scene in `docs/walkthrough/sovereign-books-walkthrough.mp4`; the
narration below is the voice track and the on-screen caption.

## Stage 1: The problem (0:00 to 0:15)

Caption: Your books should run themselves.

Narration: "QuickBooks makes great reports. It does not do your books,
and it does not tell you that the dinner you bought the whole team was
fully deductible while the one with your co-founder was only half.
Sovereign Books runs the ledger as agents and brings you the exceptions."

## Stage 2: Morning, three cards (0:15 to 0:45)

Screen: Home, then Approvals.

Narration: "Every morning the agents categorize what landed, post the
entries, and keep the trial balance clean. What they cannot decide alone
becomes a card. Today that is three. A $4,200 charge the agent is 74%
sure is software: swipe right, and it learns the rule. The deductible
share is already on the card."

## Stage 3: The tax question (0:45 to 1:10)

Screen: Approvals, meal card with answer chips.

Narration: "Here is the part QuickBooks skips. A $186 dinner at Bistro
42. Who was there? Pick 'whole company event' and it is 100% deductible
under Section 274(e)(4). Pick 'one employee' and it is 50%. Pick 'just
me' and it is nothing. One tap, and the classification, the Code
section, and the form line are recorded."

## Stage 4: The credit the agent found (1:10 to 1:35)

Screen: Approvals, tax credit card.

Narration: "The agents also watched engineering payroll and cloud spend
all quarter. Sixty-one thousand dollars qualifies for the federal
research credit. The evidence is already assembled: commits, people,
spend. Approve, and the claim file builds itself for your accountant."

## Stage 5: Go do this (1:35 to 2:05)

Screen: Credits tab with "Go do this next" and the opportunity list.

Narration: "The Credits tab turns the rules into a to-do list, nearest
win first. Hold a company-wide event before December 31. Separate food
from entertainment. Start a retirement plan for a $5,500 credit. Each
item shows the dollar value, the status, and the section of the Code."

## Stage 6: Runway, Pro, and the desktop (2:05 to 2:30)

Screen: Home runway tile, Pro tab, then the web console Tax credits
page showing the same decisions.

Narration: "Runway moves up when the credit is approved. Pro unlocks
unlimited approvals and the credit engine through Google Play. And
everything you decide on the phone shows up on the desktop console,
with the audit trail. Your finances, lowering the cost of running your
company."

## Closing card

Caption: Sovereign Books. Approve the exceptions. Pocket the credits.
