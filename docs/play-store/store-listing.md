## Full description (4000 chars max)

Sovereign Books is bookkeeping for founders who never want to open a ledger.

Agents categorize every transaction, post the journal entries, and keep the trial balance clean. When they are not sure, you get a card. Swipe right to approve, left to skip. Most mornings that is three cards and ninety seconds.

WHAT THE AGENTS DO

• Categorize transactions as they land and learn your rules from each approval
• Keep a double-entry ledger with a balanced trial balance
• Watch cash and runway so you always know how many months you have
• Draft follow-ups for overdue invoices so you only have to tap Send

THE PART QUICKBOOKS DOES NOT DO

Sovereign Books looks at engineering payroll and cloud spend as it happens and flags what qualifies for the federal Section 41 research credit and state research credits. It assembles the evidence (projects, people, spend) into a claim file your accountant can sign off on. Your finances lower the cost of running your company.

PRO

Free covers five approvals a day. Pro unlocks unlimited approvals, the tax credit engine, runway alerts, and accountant export. Subscriptions are billed through Google Play and can be cancelled anytime.

BRING YOUR OWN BACKEND

The app talks to the open-source Sovereign Books API. Point it at your own server from Settings, or use the built-in sample data to explore.

Estimates are estimates. Credits are claimed on your tax return by a professional; the app assembles the evidence.
